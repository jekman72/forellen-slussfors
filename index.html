The fishing

Big char, wild trout and clear northern water

Slussfors sits where the Ume River runs into Lake Storuman. The Kirjesån stream, known for its trout, joins the river just around the corner from the house, and the lakes Järvsjön, Magasjön and Boksjön are a short drive away.

One reason for the exceptional fishing is the char farm in Sparmanselet, about 4 km upstream from the house (★ on the map). It adds nutrients to the water, which helps keep many large char and whitefish in a small area, summer and winter. It is a big part of what brings anglers from far away.

Boats

We do not rent out a boat ourselves. Boats with or without motor can be rented locally: ask the Slussfors fishing association, contact Daniel Bergfors, +46 730 26 68 71, or look for private boats in Storuman municipality on Hygglo. You can also ask at the village shop Slussforsboa, +46 951 501 70. There is a public boat ramp in the village, and fuel is sold at the village petrol station.

Bait & tackle

Worms and maggots are usually sold at the village shop Slussforsboa, a few minutes from the house, along with a small range of fishing tackle.

Shore or boat

You can fish from shore at the point by the bridge near the char farm, at the river outlet close to the house and along the Kirjesån stream. A boat is an advantage outside the char farm, in the middle of the river, in Rännan and in the deep part of Järvsjön. Boat launches: Slussfors, Järvsjön, Magasjön and Skansnässjön. The small dots on the map show each spot.

Smoker, freezer and drying

The brick smoker in the yard is free to use, just bring your own wood. There is a freezer indoors for your catch, and in winter the fish keep well outside in boxes, since it is well below freezing. There is no dedicated drying room, but flotation suits and other gear can be dried in the hall by the radiators and in the bathroom.

Signature fish

Arctic char

Röding · Salvelinus alpinus

The star of Slussfors. Best through the ice from February to April, when the days are long and bright, and by boat from June to September. Try trolling or jigging in summer.

Brown trout

Öring · Salmo trutta

Wild trout in the Kirjesån stream (own licence, see above) and the river. Best from June to September: fly, spinner or a quiet drift at dusk in midsummer.

Grayling

Harr · Thymallus thymallus

The "lady of the stream", with her purple sail fin. Best from June to October, when she rises to small dry flies in the river currents.

Winter jigging

Whitefish

Sik · Coregonus lavaretus

Best through the ice from January to April, and in open water from June to August. Very large whitefish take small jigs on light line, so bring patience for a sunny March day.
