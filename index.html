The fishing

Big char, wild trout and clear northern water

Slussfors sits where the Ume River runs into Lake Storuman. The Kirjesån stream, known for its trout, joins the river just around the corner from the house, and the lakes Järvsjön, Magasjön and Boksjön are a short drive away.

A char farm in the area feeds the water, so there are many large fish in a fairly small area. The fishing association describes the char fishing as uniquely good in both summer and winter, with very large whitefish on the ice.

Where you'll be

On the Blue Highway, in the middle of the water

Slussfors lies on the E12 between Storuman and the mountains. Switch between the nearby fishing waters and the wider region, and tap a place to fly there.

    Warm, simple and made for long days outdoors

    80 m² with a fully equipped kitchen, a living room with an open fireplace and a big TV, and two bedrooms. Dry your gear, light the fire, and smoke today's catch in the brick smoker in the yard.

    The house is one half of a semi-detached building with its own entrance, its own yard and parking for several cars. A groomed exercise trail of about 2.5 km for walking and skiing starts right behind the house.
